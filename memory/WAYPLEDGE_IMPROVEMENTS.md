# WayPledge Suggested Improvements

**Created:** May 2025  
**Last Updated:** May 2025  
**Status:** For Review

---

## HIGH PRIORITY IMPROVEMENTS

### 1. Inspiring Prompts & Suggestions
- **Current:** Users see empty create forms without guidance
- **Suggested:** Add contextual prompts throughout the app:
  - On Browse: "What could you offer today?" / "Need something? Just ask"
  - On Create Pledge: "What do you need to clear out?" / "What skill could you share?"
  - On Create Wish: "What would make your day easier?" / "What do you need help with?"
  - Rotating inspiration cards on home screen
- **Why:** Helps users think of what to contribute; reduces blank page anxiety
- **Status:** ✅ IMPLEMENTED

### 2. Onboarding Flow Enhancement
- **Current:** Users jump straight into the app after signup
- **Suggested:** Add a 3-4 screen onboarding flow that explains:
  - What Pledges are (with visual example)
  - What Wishes are (with visual example)
  - How to find/join a Community (Hive)
  - The Do No Harm pledge concept
- **Why:** New users may feel lost without understanding the unique WayPledge philosophy
- **Status:** ✅ IMPLEMENTED

### 3. Home/Dashboard Screen
- **Current:** Browse tab shows all pledges/wishes immediately
- **Suggested:** Create a true "Home" dashboard showing:
  - Your active pledges count
  - Your active wishes count
  - Recent activity in your communities
  - "Getting Started" prompts for new users
  - Quick stats (connections made, gratitude received)
- **Why:** Gives users a personal overview before browsing

### 3. Community (Hive) Discovery
- **Current:** Communities accessed via Profile → My Communities
- **Suggested:** 
  - Add prominent "Find Your Community" on home/browse
  - Show nearby communities based on location
  - Featured/active communities section
  - Clear explanation that Hives = Communities (same thing)
- **Why:** Communities are core to WayPledge but feel hidden

### 4. Activity Feed
- **Current:** No central activity feed
- **Suggested:** Global or community-specific feed showing:
  - New pledges in your communities
  - New wishes in your communities
  - Successful connections/completions
  - Gratitude wall highlights
- **Why:** Creates sense of movement and life in the app
- **Status:** ✅ IMPLEMENTED

## MEDIUM PRIORITY IMPROVEMENTS

### 5. Post to Communities - Make More Prominent
- **Current:** "Post to Communities (optional)" exists but is easy to miss
- **Suggested:**
  - Make it more prominent/visible in the create flow
  - Better prompt: "Share with your communities?"
  - Auto-suggest their communities if they have any
  - Clearer explanation of what happens if they don't select communities
- **Why:** Communities are core to WayPledge; encouraging community posting builds connection

### 6. Visual Storytelling
- **Current:** Text-heavy explanations
- **Suggested:** Add illustrations/icons throughout that show:
  - The circle of giving/receiving
  - The honeycomb network concept
  - Success stories with images
- **Why:** The philosophy is beautiful but needs visual support

### 6. Notification Improvements  
- **Current:** Basic push notifications
- **Suggested:**
  - "Someone nearby can help with your wish"
  - "Your pledge matches someone's wish"
  - Weekly digest of community activity
  - Gentle reminders for inactive pledges
- **Why:** Keeps users engaged and connected

### 7. Search & Filter Enhancement
- **Current:** Basic search by text
- **Suggested:**
  - Location-based search ("within 5 miles")
  - Time-based filter ("posted this week")
  - Skills/category browser
  - "Random act of kindness" - show random wish to help
- **Why:** Makes finding relevant pledges/wishes easier

### 8. Profile Enhancement
- **Current:** Basic profile with bio
- **Suggested:**
  - Skills/offerings summary
  - Gratitude received counter
  - Communities joined badges
  - "Trusted Member" indicators
  - Personal impact stats
- **Why:** Builds trust and showcases contribution
